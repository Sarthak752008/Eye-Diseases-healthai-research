Guided Preparation & Protocol

Follow these 6 simple steps for optimal optical clarity, non-invasive imaging, and reliable screening results.

1

Diffuse Illumination

Face an indirect, soft light source. Avoid direct flash, harsh sunlight reflections, or heavy shadows on the ocular surface.

2

Enable Camera

Allow browser camera permissions when prompted. Alternatively, use research photograph file upload.

3

Eye Positioning (10–15 cm)

Position the target eye within 10–15 cm of the lens. Macro anterior view required, NOT a distant full-face selfie.

4

Non-Invasive View Selection

Gently expose the lower palpebral conjunctiva by resting a clean fingertip on the lower cheekbone. Never force or pull.

5

Automated Quality Check

The system checks sharpness and luminance in real-time. If blur or glare is detected, instant feedback is provided.

6

Downloadable 2-Page Report

Receive an official, two-page clinical research report in English or Hindi with complete diagnostic safety boundaries.
