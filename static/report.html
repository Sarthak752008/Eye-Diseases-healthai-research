💡 What This Means & Understanding Your Confidence Score

This screening evaluates surface optical color patterns from the mucosal lining of your lower eyelid and the white protective surface of your eye. While eye pallor and redness provide valuable screening signals, this non-invasive test is not a medical diagnosis.

How to interpret confidence: The model's confidence indicates algorithmic consistency with research training data. It never represents the probability that you have a disease. Room lighting, camera white balance, and skin undertones influence color readings, which is why laboratory confirmation is always recommended.

🩺 TREATMENT & SCREENING RECOMMENDATIONS: Recommended Next Steps (Your Action Plan)

1. Discuss With Your Doctor Share this 2-page report with your physician during a routine check-up. The second page contains technical metrics specifically designed for healthcare professionals.
